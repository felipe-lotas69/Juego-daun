Juego Daun

Three games that run in a browser tab. No install, no account, nothing uploaded anywhere.

  • Chromewood

    An isometric pixel-art survival RPG. You land with nothing: chop, mine, cook, farm, build a camp, and go down the caves for the metal. Procedurally generated island, skill tree, eight kinds of night, and no objective you have to take.

    Solo, or up to four in co-op · needs WebGL2

  • Getaway Daun

    A physics-based lean-and-hop action platformer. Grab a gun, ride the elevators, smash the glass, reach the van. You can't walk — you lean, you hop, you pray.

    Campaign and versus · keyboard

  • Getaway Daun

    The rebuilt one. A ragdoll race down a high street, over the rooftops, along a freight train and up through a night office, with guns whose recoil is how you cross the big gaps. You cannot walk: you lean, you let go, you pray.

    Up to four at one keyboard, or against bots

Chromewood's co-op is peer to peer: one person hosts, the others type in the room code, and the game data goes straight between browsers. Nothing is stored on a server.
