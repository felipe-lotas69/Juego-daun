CONTROLS

ADLean left / right. On the ground a lean turns into a hop — that's how you move.
WJump. You launch in the direction you are leaning. Stand tall for height, lean hard for distance.
RInteract: pick up guns, call elevators, slam buttons. With nothing in reach it fires your weapon.
SPACEFire. Recoil pushes you — shotguns and rockets are movement tools.
QDrop the weapon you're holding.
PESCPause.
ENTERRestart the level (campaign), or start the next round (versus).
